Employee handbook

Run the late shift.

Fryday is open until 2am. The regulars are hungry, the grill is hot, and Nugget is technically the manager.

01
Cook the patties

Tap the burger or press Space. Every tap fills the oldest ticket. Upgrades add auto-grill output.

02
Cash the tickets

When a ticket fills, it pays out automatically. Keep the queue moving before it backs up.

03
Stack the sizzle

Fast tapping heats the meter and multiplies every ticket payout. It cools down when you stop.

04
Send in the corgi

Tap Nugget for Zoomies: double output for a short burst. Pet treats make it last longer and hit harder.
