Gravekeeper handbook

Run the midnight shift.

The Graveyard Grill is open until dawn. The regulars are restless, the hellfire is hot, and Nugget is technically the manager.

01

Tap the burger or press Space. Every tap fills the oldest ticket. Upgrades add haunted auto-grill output.

02
Cash the souls

When a ticket fills, it pays out automatically. Keep the queue moving before the dead get impatient.

03

Fast tapping heats the meter and multiplies every ticket payout. It cools down when you stop.
